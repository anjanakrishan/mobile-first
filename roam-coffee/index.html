Our Brand Story

From Central Coast California to Bowmanville

Roam Coffee started as a dream over six years ago in Central Coast California. Now serving the Durham Region, we strive to bring the excellence and culture of Californian third-wave coffee to our beautiful Ontario home.

We are dedicated to coffee quality and sustainability, partnering with some of the best Canadian roasters and producers to bring you a local and environmentally-conscious cup.
